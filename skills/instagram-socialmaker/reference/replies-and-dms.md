# Replies to comments and DMs

## Comments under your own posts

Sort them first, then answer in this order:

1. **Keyword comments** (they asked for the thing): deliver by DM (automation) + short public reply.
2. **Leads** ("how much?", "do you ship to…?", "available Saturday?"): answer publicly in one line and move the details to DM.
3. **Real questions:** answer in public, fully. Others have the same question.
4. **Support / praise:** a like and 3-8 words, varied. No copy-paste.
5. **Noise or spam:** ignore or hide.

Reply in the first hour when you can: early replies keep the post active.

## DM templates

**Price question**
- ES: «¡Hola, {nombre}! {Servicio} cuesta {precio} e incluye {qué}. Si quieres, aquí eliges día y hora: {enlace}»
- EN: "Hi {name}! {Service} is {price} and includes {what}. If you'd like, pick a day and time here: {link}"
- PT: «Oi, {nome}! {Serviço} sai R$ {valor} e inclui {o quê}. Pode ser no Pix ou no cartão. Se quiser, escolhe o dia e o horário aqui: {link}»
- FR: «Bonjour {prénom} ! {Prestation} : {prix} €, {ce qui est inclus}. Vous pouvez réserver votre créneau ici : {lien}»

**"Do you have availability?"**
- ES: «¡Sí! Estos son mis horarios libres: {enlace}. Reservas en un minuto.»
- EN: "Yes! Here are my open times: {link}. Booking takes a minute."
- PT: «Tenho sim! Esses são os horários livres: {link}. Leva um minutinho pra agendar.»
- FR: «Oui ! Voici les créneaux disponibles : {lien}. La réservation prend une minute.»

**Follow-up (once, 1-3 days later, only if they asked first)**
- ES: «Hola, {nombre}, ¿pudiste ver los horarios? Si te queda mejor otro día, dime y lo vemos.»
- EN: "Hi {name}, did you get a chance to see the times? If another day works better, tell me."
- PT: «Oi, {nome}! Conseguiu ver os horários? Se outro dia for melhor pra você, me fala que a gente vê.»
- FR: «Bonjour {prénom}, avez-vous pu regarder les créneaux ? Si un autre jour vous arrange mieux, dites-le-moi.»

**Collab pitch (to a specific account, with a reason)**
- ES: «Hola, {nombre}. Vi tu reel sobre {tema} y {algo concreto que te gustó}. Tengo una idea: {propuesta en una línea}. ¿Te interesa que te cuente?»
- EN: "Hi {name}. I saw your reel about {topic} and {one specific thing}. Idea: {one-line proposal}. Want me to send details?"
- PT: «Oi, {nome}! Vi seu reel sobre {tema} e {algo concreto}. Tive uma ideia: {proposta em uma linha}. Posso te contar?»
- FR: «Bonjour {prénom}, j'ai vu votre Reel sur {sujet} et {un détail précis}. J'ai une idée : {proposition en une ligne}. Je vous en dis plus ?»

If the collaboration is paid or gifted, the post must say so: #publi in Brazil, «Publicité» or «Collaboration commerciale» in France.

## Rules

- Two to four sentences. A wall of text gets ignored.
- Give before you ask: the price, the link, the answer.
- One follow-up maximum. Never DM people who never wrote to you.
- If something repeats every week (price, schedule, menu), it should be an automation, not a copy-paste: see [automations.md](automations.md).
