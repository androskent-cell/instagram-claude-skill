# Stories

Stories are where followers become conversations. The feed finds new people; stories warm up the ones you already have.

## A daily sequence (4-6 stories)

1. **Hook story:** a question or a behind-the-scenes moment.
2. **Context:** what you are working on today, with a real photo or video.
3. **Interaction:** a poll, a quiz or a question sticker about it.
4. **Value:** one tip, one result, one client story (with permission).
5. **Invitation:** «Respóndeme AGENDA y te paso horarios» / "Reply BOOK and I'll send you times" / «Responde AGENDA que eu te mando os horários» / «Répondez « RDV » et je vous envoie les créneaux», or the link sticker.

## Which sticker does which job

| Sticker | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| Poll | Get a first tap from shy followers (easy yes/no) |
| Question box | Collect real questions for your next Reel |
| Quiz | Teach something in a playful way |
| Link | Send them to your link in bio page, booking page or catalog |
| Slider (emoji) | Measure interest before a launch |

## Story replies → DMs

Every reply to a story opens a DM. With an automation on story replies (see [automations.md](automations.md)), a word like AGENDA or GUÍA can answer instantly with the link. Pin the best sequences in Highlights: "Precios", "Reservas", "Clientes" («Preços», «Agenda»; «Tarifs», «Réserver»). In France and Brazil a prices highlight also keeps the price visible, as the law asks.

If a brand paid for the story, the label stays on screen the whole time: #publi in Brazil, «Publicité» or «Collaboration commerciale» in France.
