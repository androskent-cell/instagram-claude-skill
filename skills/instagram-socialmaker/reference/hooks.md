# Hooks: the first second decides everything

A hook is the first line spoken and the first text on screen. It has one job: make the right person stay. Write 3 options, score them with the checklist, keep the best, and say why.

## Checklist (score each 0-2, keep 8+)

1. **Concrete:** a number, a name, a price, a place, a time. ("3 mistakes" beats "some mistakes".)
2. **Who it's for:** the viewer recognizes themselves in it.
3. **Stakes:** something to gain or lose.
4. **Fast:** under 3 seconds out loud (about 8-10 words).
5. **Honest:** the video delivers exactly what the hook promises.

Kill on sight: greetings ("hola chicos", "hey guys"), "in today's video", "stop scrolling", questions answered with "no", and anything the video does not deliver.

## 16 formulas, in both languages

| # | Formula | ES | EN |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Specific mistake | «El error que hace que nadie te pida precio» | "The mistake that makes nobody ask your price" |
| 2 | Number + result | «3 mensajes que me trajeron 12 citas esta semana» | "3 DMs that booked me 12 appointments this week" |
| 3 | Before / after | «Antes contestaba 80 mensajes a mano. Ahora, cero.» | "I used to answer 80 DMs by hand. Now, zero." |
| 4 | Nobody tells you | «Nadie te dice esto cuando abres tu negocio en Instagram» | "Nobody tells you this when you start selling on Instagram" |
| 5 | You're doing X | «Si pones "precio por inbox", estás perdiendo ventas» | "If you write 'DM for price', you're losing sales" |
| 6 | The exact thing | «Este es el mensaje exacto que mando cuando alguien pregunta "¿cuánto?"» | "This is the exact message I send when someone asks 'how much?'" |
| 7 | Steal this | «Copia esta bio, solo cambia tu ciudad» | "Steal this bio, just change the city" |
| 8 | Myth | «No necesitas 10 000 seguidores para vender» | "You don't need 10,000 followers to sell" |
| 9 | Price reveal | «Esto cuesta montar una agenda de citas (spoiler: $0)» | "What a booking page actually costs (spoiler: $0)" |
| 10 | Story with stakes | «Perdí una clienta por contestar al día siguiente» | "I lost a client because I replied the next day" |
| 11 | Ranking | «Del peor al mejor: así responden 5 negocios a "¿tienes horario?"» | "Worst to best: how 5 businesses answer 'any openings?'" |
| 12 | Challenge | «Intenté no contestar un solo DM a mano por 7 días» | "I tried not answering a single DM by hand for 7 days" |
| 13 | Local + specific | «Si vendes en Miami y tus clientes hablan español, mira esto» | "If you sell in Austin and half your clients speak Spanish, watch this" |
| 14 | Contrarian | «Deja de publicar todos los días» | "Stop posting every day" |
| 15 | Question with a cost | «¿Cuántas citas pierdes por no contestar de noche?» | "How many bookings do you lose by not replying at night?" |
| 16 | Show, then explain | (on screen: the result) «Así llegó este pedido. Te explico.» | (on screen: the result) "This order came in by itself. Here's how." |

## Adapt to the niche

Swap the nouns, keep the shape. Real estate: "showings", "listings". English teacher: "trial class", "students from Brazil". Beauty: "appointments", "no-shows". Creators: "brand deals", "your guide".

## Output format

```
1. «hook»: 9/10 (concrete + stakes; weakest: who it's for)
2. ...
Best: #1, because…
On-screen text (max 6 words): …
```
