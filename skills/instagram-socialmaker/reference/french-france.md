# French (France): sober, concrete, and the right "tu" or "vous"

Read this before writing anything in French. Written for France, with notes for Belgium, Switzerland and Quebec at the end. French audiences distrust hype: 70% of French people have a bad image of influencers (Cetelem / Harris Interactive, 2023). Proof, prices and plain talk sell; salesman enthusiasm sounds like a scam.

## Tu or vous: decide once, keep it everywhere

- Default for captions, Reels and carousels: the collective **vous**, talking to the whole community: «Je vous montre…», «Dites-moi en commentaire», «Enregistrez ce post». Natural at any age.
- **tu** fits creators and young niches («Si tu galères avec…», «Réserve ton créneau»). Once chosen, use it in the Reel, the caption, the replies and the automatic DMs. A Reel in tu and a DM in vous feels off.
- DMs: if you know nothing about the person, start with vous and mirror them (if they write tu, use tu from the next reply).
- Save it in the voice note: `Registre: tu | vous` (see [voice.md](voice.md)).

| Business | Register |
| --- | --- |
| Real estate agent | vous everywhere (big money, clients aged 30 to 60) |
| English teacher online | tu for students and young adults; vous for professionals, companies or CPF-funded courses |
| Nails, beauty, institut | tu is common with young clients in Reels; high-end uses vous; booking DMs in vous until the client says tu |
| Barber | tu |
| Caterer (traiteur) | vous |
| Shop, crafts | vous; tu for a young audience |
| Coach | tu in fitness and personal growth; vous in executive coaching |

Avoid inclusive writing with the middle dot («client·e·s») in sales copy: use «celles et ceux» or «toutes les personnes qui…».

## Tone

- Concrete beats adjectives: a price, a time, a place, a name, a number.
- Franc-parler, dry humor and self-mockery work; salesman enthusiasm does not.
- One exclamation mark at most per sentence, and not in every sentence. Never «!!!».
- Tell the savoir-faire: «fait maison», «fabriqué à Lyon», «artisan».
- Comment replies: short, personal, with the name or a detail. Never the same reply pasted everywhere.

## Calls to action

| Goal | tu | vous |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Save | Enregistre ce post pour le retrouver plus tard. | Enregistrez ce post pour le retrouver plus tard. |
| Send (reaches new people) | Envoie-le à quelqu'un qui doit absolument le voir. | Envoyez-le à la personne qui cherche un appart en ce moment. |
| Comment | Et toi, tu fais comment ? Dis-le-moi en commentaire. | Et vous, vous préférez quoi ? Dites-le-moi en commentaire. |
| Keyword to DM | Commente « TARIFS » et je t'envoie la grille en privé. | Commentez « VISITE » et je vous envoie les infos en message privé. |
| Booking | Le lien pour réserver est dans ma bio. | Réservez votre créneau en ligne : lien en bio. |
| Follow | Abonne-toi si tu veux la partie 2. | Abonnez-vous pour ne pas rater la suite. |

- Keywords: short, no accent, no ambiguity: GUIDE, PRIX, RDV, MENU, VISITE, TARIFS.
- «en privé» is the most neutral; «en DM» and «en MP» are common too.
- Beauty clients in France are used to booking online with time slots (Planity equips about a quarter of beauty businesses). The natural call is «Réservez en ligne (lien en bio)» with the booking page there, not «écris-moi pour un créneau».
- Public replies: «C'est envoyé en privé !», «Je t'ai envoyé le guide, regarde tes messages.», «Je vous l'ai envoyé en message privé.»
- Avoid: «Prix en DM / en MP» (the price must be visible, see below), «Dernière chance !!!», «Places limitées, fonce !» (if false, it is a misleading practice), «Tague 3 amis», «Like si tu es d'accord», «Go en DM», «Qui est chaud ?», «Offre exceptionnelle», «N'hésitez pas à me contacter» (write «Écrivez-moi»), WHOLE POSTS IN CAPITALS.

## Rules the skill must follow in France

Not legal advice. Before publishing legal text (rules of a contest, legal notices), check it on Legifrance.

1. **Price visible, TTC.** Every seller must show the price before the sale, on every channel, so the client knows it «sans avoir à le demander», in euros including tax (TTC). Write «À partir de 45 €» in the caption, keep a highlight «Tarifs» and a catalog with prices. For custom work (catering for events): «sur devis», saying what changes the price and whether the quote is free. A discount shows the reference price (the price of the previous 30 days). Fines go up to 3,000 € for an individual and 15,000 € for a company.
2. **Commercial influence (law 2023-451).** When a business pays, gifts or gives a commission to a creator, the post says «Publicité» or «Collaboration commerciale» (or uses Instagram's label), visible without a click, naming the brand. A shop promoting its own product on its own account does not need it: the context makes it clear.
3. **Retouched or AI images.** In commercial influence: «Images retouchées» when a face or a silhouette was modified, «Images virtuelles» for an AI-generated face or silhouette. Commercial photos of models with a reshaped silhouette: «Photographie retouchée» (2017 decree).
4. **Never promoted by influencers:** cosmetic surgery, nicotine products, crypto-assets and certain financial products (with exceptions), sports betting tips, methods presented as a substitute for medical care (coaches: never).
5. **CPF (funded training, English teachers):** never message people to sell with their CPF («Utilise ton CPF») and never ask for their CPF balance by DM: prospecting CPF holders on social networks is banned. A CPF-eligible course promoted on Instagram carries the public-funding mention (2026 decree).
6. **Jeux-concours** are allowed if fair. Rules reachable from the link in bio (organizer with SIREN, dates, who can enter, prize and its value, how the winner is chosen and contacted, use of data) and, in the caption: «Ce jeu n'est ni organisé, ni sponsorisé, ni géré par Instagram». One simple action (comment an answer) and a product as the prize, not money.
7. **RGPD and AI.** The DM that answers a comment was asked for, so it is fine: deliver only what was promised. To add someone to an e-mail or WhatsApp list, ask («Tu veux recevoir nos offres par e-mail ? Réponds OUI»), say why and how to stop («Répondez STOP pour ne plus recevoir de messages»); nothing mass-sent later to people who only commented. Under the EU AI Act (art. 50, from August 2, 2026), if an AI writes the replies, say so in the first message («Vous échangez avec un assistant automatique») and offer a person. No sensitive data (health) by DM.
8. **Link in bio page:** a professional who sells needs «Mentions légales» (name, SIREN, «EI» or micro-entrepreneur, contact, host) and CGV if they take orders.

## Typography

- A space before ? ! ; (narrow no-break space, U+202F) and before : (no-break space, U+00A0). Otherwise Instagram can push the sign alone to the next line. `scripts/humanize.py` adds them to French text.
- Quotes « like this », with no-break spaces inside. Keyword call: Commentez « GUIDE ».
- Money: «45 €», «1 234,56 €», «45 € TTC». Never «€45» or «45.00€».
- Time: «18 h 30» in e-mails and formal text; «18h30» is fine in posts and stories. Never «6:30 PM». Ranges: «de 9 h à 18 h».
- Dates: «lundi 3 novembre» (lowercase), 1er, 2e, 3e (not «2ème»).
- Accented capitals: À, É, Ç («À partir de…»). Titles: only the first word gets a capital («Les 5 erreurs à éviter»).
- «20 %» with a space. Phone: «06 12 34 56 78». «M.», «Mme».
- Instagram words stay in English: story, Reel, post, DM, feed, live, «lien en bio». Office anglicisms do not: mindset, skills, level up, game changer.

## Hashtags and search

- 3 to 5: trade + city + topic, without accents: #ongleslyon #prothesisteongulaire #traiteurbordeaux. The caption keeps its accents.
- Write the first line like a Google search: «Pose gel à Lyon : combien de temps ça tient ?».
- The words the French search, not translations: prothésiste ongulaire, semi-permanent, pose américaine, dégradé américain, traiteur mariage, plateau apéro, chasseur immobilier, estimation gratuite, primo-accédant, cours d'anglais en ligne, coach sportif à domicile, fait main.
- Name field: «Léa | Ongles Lyon 7e».

## Payments, WhatsApp and bookings

- Card dominates; instant bank transfer is free; Wero (it replaced Paylib) and Lydia (young people); cash in the shop. Never Pix or Mercado Pago in French texts.
- For bookings and catering, an «acompte» (deposit) is normal: say it next to the price.
- WhatsApp is on almost every phone, but writing to a business on WhatsApp is less common than in Latin America or Brazil. Use it as an order button (caterer, crafts, small shops) with the message pre-filled, never to send promotions without consent.
- Real estate: Instagram builds the personal brand and finds sellers; the DM leads to «estimation offerte».

## French dates that sell

La rentrée (September) · les soldes (regulated dates, winter and summer) · Galette des rois (January) · Saint-Valentin (14/2) · Fête des mères (usually the last Sunday of May; check each year) · Black Friday · Noël. Many shops close in August.

## Belgium, Switzerland, Quebec

- **Belgium:** like France; paid content recognizable at first sight (#publicité or «collaboration commerciale»). «septante», «nonante», «GSM».
- **Switzerland:** vous by default in commerce; «CHF 1'234.50» or «1'234 fr. 50»; «septante», «nonante» («huitante» in Vaud, Valais and Fribourg); «natel».
- **Quebec:** tu is much more common from the first contact, so tu by default on Instagram; no space before ? ! ; (the space before : stays); «1 234,56 $»; «courriel», «cellulaire».
