# Reels: one idea, one script, one call to action

## Before writing, get (in one question)

1. The one idea of the video, or the problem the viewer has.
2. Length target: 15, 30 or 60 seconds (default 30).
3. What the viewer should do at the end: comment a word, save, visit the link, book.

## Script shape

| Part | Time | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 0-3 s | The line from [hooks.md](hooks.md) + on-screen text |
| Proof or setup | 3-8 s | Why listen to you: a result, a number, a mistake you made |
| The value | 8-25 s | 2-4 short beats. Change the shot or the on-screen text every beat |
| The CTA | last 3-5 s | One action. Ideally a keyword: «Comenta AGENDA y te mando el enlace» / "Comment BOOK and I'll send you the link" / «Comenta AGENDA que eu te mando o link» / «Commentez « RDV » et je vous envoie le lien» |

Speaking pace: about 150-165 words per minute. A 30-second Reel is 70-80 words. If the script is longer, cut, do not speed up.

## Write it like this

```
HOOK (0:00) «…»                         [on screen: …]
BEAT 1 (0:03) «…»                       [b-roll: …]
BEAT 2 (0:09) «…»                       [on screen: …]
BEAT 3 (0:16) «…»
CTA (0:25) «Comenta GUÍA y te la mando» [on screen: Comenta GUÍA]
Caption: (see captions.md)
```

## Rules

- One idea. If you need "and also", it is two Reels.
- Every beat needs something concrete: a number, an example, a screen.
- End where the hook started (same word or number) so the loop feels natural.
- On-screen text: max 6 words per card, high contrast, away from the bottom 20% (the caption covers it).
- Captions/subtitles on: many people watch muted.

## If the CTA is a keyword

The keyword only works if something answers it. Pair every keyword Reel with an automation from [automations.md](automations.md). In Build mode, offer to create it right after the script.
