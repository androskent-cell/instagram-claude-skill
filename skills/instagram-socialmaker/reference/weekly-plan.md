# Weekly plan

Consistency beats volume. A small business does well with 3 feed posts and daily stories.

## Ask first (one question)

What they sell, who buys, their goal this month (followers, leads, bookings, sales), and how many hours a week they have.

## Default week

| Day | Format | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | Reel | Reach: a hook-driven tip for new people |
| Wed | Carousel | Saves: a mini-guide or checklist |
| Fri | Reel or post | Conversion: offer, result, or client story with a keyword CTA |
| Daily | 4-6 stories | Warm up followers, polls, behind the scenes |

Mix of topics over a month: 40% useful tips, 30% proof (results, reviews, behind the scenes), 20% personal (who you are), 10% direct offers.

## Output

For each post: day, format, hook, the one idea, CTA (and whether it needs an automation), and a 1-line note for the visual. End with the 1-2 automations that would support the week's CTAs.

## Measure next week

Look at: shares and saves (content worked), comments with the keyword (offer worked), link taps and bookings (the profile worked). Keep what got results, drop what got nothing.
